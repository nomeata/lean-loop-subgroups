# Blueprint: Loop subgroups of F_r

Formalization of J. Breitner, *Loop subgroups of F_r and the image of their stabilizer
subgroups in GL_r(Z)*, Israel J. Math. 191 (2012), 545–557,
[doi:10.1007/s11856-011-0213-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11856-011-0213-3),
[arXiv:1012.2729](https://arxiv.org/abs/1012.2729). The same material is the loop-subgroup chapter
of the Diplomarbeit (`Diplomarbeit.tex`).

Built on Mathlib and [Tau Ceti](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti). From Tau Ceti we use
`TauCeti.transvectionUnit` (elementary matrices in `GL`), the Chevalley commutator relation
`commutatorElement_transvectionUnit`, and `elementarySubgroup_eq_ker_det_of_euclideanDomain`
(`SL_n(ℤ)` is generated by elementary matrices).

Status: **everything listed below is proved.** There is no `sorry`, and only the axioms `propext`,
`Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` are used.

## Setting and conventions

* `F_r` is `FreeGroup ι` for a `Fintype ι`, so `r = Fintype.card ι`, with basis `g_i = FreeGroup.of i`.
  `LoopSubgroups/General.lean` transports the results to any group `G` with any basis
  `b : FreeGroupBasis ι G`.
* `Stab U := MulAction.stabilizer (MulAut F_r) U` uses the pointwise action of `Aut(F_r)` on
  subgroups.
* `X hij c` is `X_ij(c) = 1 + c E_ij`. The paper's `X_ij` is `X hij 1`, and its `X_ij²` is
  `X hij 2`.
* "At most `r − 2` looplets" is written `(univ.filter (s · = 1)).card + 2 ≤ card ι`.
* Most theorems take `[U.FiniteIndex]` as a hypothesis. It follows from Definition 1 when all
  `s_i ≥ 1` (`IsLoopSubgroup.finiteIndex`); `image_Stab_eq_of_isLoopSubgroup` states Theorem 1
  without it.

## Remarks on the paper found while formalizing

1. **Lemma 8 needs `r ≥ 3`.** The paper states it for every `r`. For `r = 2` and `v = (1,1)`,
   the generating set is empty, but `S(v)` contains the swap matrix. The proof itself uses a
   third index, and the paper only applies the lemma for `r ≥ 3`, so the main results are not
   affected.
2. **`τ_j ∈ Stab(U)` is not justified** in the paper; the thesis says only that `τ_j` "stabilizes
   any loop subgroup". It is true, but a proof is needed. A loop is a cycle `σ`, and some
   permutation `c` with `c σ c⁻¹ = σ⁻¹` fixes `U` and is supported on that loop. This `c`
   commutes with the other generators (`exists_conj_eq_inv`, `invAut_mem_Stab`).
3. **Lemma 5 generalised.** The formal proof is coordinate-free. If two cycles meet in exactly one
   point, every even permutation supported on the union of their supports lies in the commutator
   subgroup of `⟨σ, ω⟩`. The paper's statement in `S_n` is derived from this.
4. **Remark 17 doesn't need the characteristic kernel.** It is used only to make the induced map
   `ψ` well defined. Once `ψ` is given together with `ψ(γ)(φ x) = φ(γ x)`, surjectivity of `φ` is
   enough.
5. **Prop. 18: surjectivity of `B`.** The paper says the preimages come from "the automorphisms in
   the proof of Lemma 10". What is meant are the Nielsen moves (`w = 1`). The formal proof uses
   `g_j ↦ g_i^c g_j` together with `SL_r(ℤ) = E_r(ℤ)` from Tau Ceti.
6. Typo in the preface: "the image `B(GL_r(ℤ))`" should be "`B(Stab(U))`".
7. **Gap in Lemma `unboundstabgroup` (thesis).** The proof claims that `π(g_i)^n` is again an
   `s_i`-cycle for `1 ≤ n < s_i`. This fails when `gcd(n, s_i) > 1`. The statement is still true:
   the formal proof uses the test word `g_i⁻¹ g_j g_i ∈ U`, whose images under the powers `γ^n`
   are not in `U` for `0 < n < s_i`.
8. **Lemma `max` holds for `r ≥ 2`.** The proof goes through 2-transitivity of `GL_r(𝔽₂)` on
   nonzero vectors.
9. **CosetProject.** The formal implementation checks "row `i` already merged?" before every
   relator, not once per row as in the pseudocode. Otherwise a row merged during its own relator
   loop could reappear in the table. A random search found no input where this actually happens,
   but that is not proved. `Coincidence` uses an explicit stack instead of recursion. The converse
   direction `i ≡ j ⇒ i ≈ j` is proved via the normal subgroup `{w ∣ ∀k, f(ρ(w)k) = f(k)}`
   instead of induction on the conjugating word.
10. **Lemma `index2intersection`.** The formal proof is a closure induction instead of the
    word-based induction.
